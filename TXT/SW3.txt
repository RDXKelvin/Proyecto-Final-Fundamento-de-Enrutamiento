Objetivo del dispositivo:
El SW3 es el Switch de Acceso del Edificio 2, dedicado a los segmentos corporativos (Servidores, Empleados, Finanzas y TI). Sus puertos de acceso están protegidos con Port-Security y se conecta al Switch Multicapa (SMC1) a través de un EtherChannel LACP en sus puertos Gigabit.

Proceso de configuración:
! 4.1 Parámetros iniciales
ena
conf t
hostname SW3
banner motd #SOLO PERSONAL AUTORIZADO Kelvin De Jesus Luna 2025-2144#

! 4.2 Creación de VLANs
vlan 2
 name SERVIDORES
vlan 3
 name EMPLEADOS
vlan 4
 name FINANZAS
vlan 5
 name TI
vlan 6
 name WIFI
vlan 7
 name NATIVA
exit

! 4.3 Asignación por rangos con Port-Security y BPDU Guard
! Servidores (VLAN 2)
int ra fa0/1-3
 sw mo acc
 sw acc vlan 2
 spanning-tree portfast
 spanning-tree bpduguard enable
 sw port-security
 sw port-security maximum 2
 sw port-security violation shutdown
 sw port-security mac-address sticky
 no sh
exit

! Empleados (VLAN 3)
int ra fa0/4-8
 sw mo acc
 sw acc vlan 3
 spanning-tree portfast
 spanning-tree bpduguard enable
 sw port-security
 sw port-security maximum 2
 sw port-security violation shutdown
 sw port-security mac-address sticky
 no sh
exit

! Finanzas (VLAN 4)
int ra fa0/9-11
 sw mo acc
 sw acc vlan 4
 spanning-tree portfast
 spanning-tree bpduguard enable
 sw port-security
 sw port-security maximum 2
 sw port-security violation shutdown
 sw port-security mac-address sticky
 no sh
exit

! TI (VLAN 5)
int ra fa0/12-13
 sw mo acc
 sw acc vlan 5
 spanning-tree portfast
 spanning-tree bpduguard enable
 sw port-security
 sw port-security maximum 2
 sw port-security violation shutdown
 sw port-security mac-address sticky
 no sh
exit

! Puertos de reserva protegidos
int ra fa0/14-18
 sw mo acc
 spanning-tree portfast
 spanning-tree bpduguard enable
 sw port-security
 sw port-security maximum 2
 sw port-security violation shutdown
 sw port-security mac-address sticky
 no sh
exit

! 4.4 EtherChannel LACP hacia SMC1
int port-channel 2
 sw mo tru
 sw tru native vlan 7
 sw tru allowed vlan 2,3,4,5,6,7
 sw nonegotiate
 no sh
exit
int ra gig0/1-2
 sw mo tru
 sw tru native vlan 7
 sw tru allowed vlan 2,3,4,5,6,7
 channel-group 2 mode active
 no sh
exit

! 4.5 Guardado
end
wr
